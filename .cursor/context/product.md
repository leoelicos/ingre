# Product

Shopping app for the home cook. Recipes come first; the list is the ingredients those recipes need.

Live site: https://ingre.vercel.app/

Header: logo, search and the account icon only. Log in and Sign up live in the account icon menu; Saved appears there after sign-in.

Hearts and the shopping list stay on this device. No login is required to save a recipe or shop. Login, signup, and Stripe upgrade still exist for accounts.

Search is in the header. Tap Off (`/tapoff`) is a route, not a header link. Tap Off is reached from the shop step.

There is no Edamam client. Recipes and ingredients are JSON in the client.
